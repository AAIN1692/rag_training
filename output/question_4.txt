ANSWER:
--------
Based on the provided employee handbooks, the rules for business travel reimbursements are as follows:

**Per Diem**
*   **Coverage:** Per diem is provided for food expenses, tips, and other incidental expenses. No bills or receipts need to be submitted for this claim (**Offshore Handbook, Page 7; Onsite Handbook, Page 8**).
*   **Onsite Rates:** For onsite business travel, the per diem is $250 per day (for 24 hours) for Tier 1 cities and $200 per day (for 24 hours) for Tier 2 cities. This amount includes accommodation (**Onsite Handbook, Page 7**).

**Accommodation and Airfare**
*   **Booking:** The travel team is responsible for obtaining the lowest available fares and lodging expenses.
*   **Approvals:** If an employee needs to stay at a hotel or book an airfare more expensive than the designated/lowest option, they must provide proper justification before booking.
    *   In the **Offshore Handbook**, this requires approval from a **VP/SVP** (**Page 7**).
    *   In the **Onsite Handbook**, this requires approval from the **Department Head** (**Page 8**).

**General Reimbursement Rules**
*   **Receipts:** Original receipts are required for all expenses except for per diems. This includes original boarding passes for airplane/train travel, credit card receipts, and detailed merchant receipts (**Onsite Handbook, Page 50**).
*   **Documentation:** Receipts must be accompanied by a summary outlining:
    *   The nature of the expense.
    *   The name and titles of the individuals involved.
    *   The purpose of the expenses (**Onsite Handbook, Page 50**).
*   **Cancellations:** If there is a change in plan or a cancellation, the employee is responsible for keeping all parties informed (**Offshore Handbook, Page 7; Onsite Handbook, Page 8**).
