ANSWER:
----------
Based on the provided employee handbooks:

**Can employees take leave during the notice period?**
No. Both handbooks state that employees are not eligible for any leaves while serving the notice period (Offshore Handbook, Page 12; Onsite Handbook, Page 18).

**What happens if they take leave?**
*   **Offshore Employees:** In the event of an emergency where an employee must take leave, they will be required to serve the number of days taken as leave (Source: Offshore Handbook, Page 12).
*   **Onsite Employees:** While the handbook states employees are not eligible for leave, it specifies that if an early release is requested by the employee and granted at management's discretion, EL (Earned Leave) will be adjusted against the early days (Source: Onsite Handbook, Page 18).
