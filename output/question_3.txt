ANSWER:
--------
According to the provided handbooks, an employee must repay relocation costs under the following conditions:

*   **Voluntary Termination:** If an employee decides to voluntarily terminate their services from the company before completing 6 months, they are required to repay the relocation costs claimed (Offshore Handbook, page 9; Onsite Handbook, page 10).
*   **Timeframe:** Specifically, the repayment is required if the employee terminates their services with Affine within 6 months of relocating (Offshore Handbook, page 9).
