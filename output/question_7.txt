Based on the provided employee handbook context, here is the comparison of business travel policies:

### 1. Onsite Policy
*   **Per diem:** $250 per day (24 hours) for Tier 1 cities and $200 per day (24 hours) for Tier 2 cities. This amount includes accommodation and is provided for food, tips, and other incidental expenses.
*   **Accommodation:** Included in the per diem rate. For specific travel (e.g., Singapore), hotel accommodation is provided for 2 weeks from the date of arrival.
*   **Travel booking:** The Affine travel team is responsible for obtaining the lowest airfares and lodging expenses available.
*   **Approval requirements:** If a hotel or airfare more expensive than the designated lowest fare is required, proper justification is needed and must be approved by the **Department Head** before booking. Stay extensions must be approved by the **EVP** in writing.
*   **Reimbursement:** Follows the "Procedure to claim reimbursements for internal travels and expenditures." A $600 advance is provided as a security deposit for hotel accommodation in specific contexts (e.g., Singapore).
*   **Expense/receipt requirements:** No bills or receipts need to be submitted for per diem claims.

### 2. Offshore Policy
*   **Per diem:** Not specified in the retrieved context (the specific dollar amount is missing). However, it is noted that per diem is provided for food, tips, and incidental expenses.
*   **Accommodation:** The travel team is responsible for obtaining the lowest lodging expenses.
*   **Travel booking:** The Affine travel team is responsible for obtaining the lowest airfares and lodging expenses available.
*   **Approval requirements:** If a hotel or airfare more expensive than the designated lowest fare is required, proper justification is needed and must be approved by a **VP/SVP** before booking. Hotel extensions must be approved by the **Function Head** in writing.
*   **Reimbursement:** Follows the "Procedure to claim reimbursements for internal travels and expenditures."
*   **Expense/receipt requirements:** No bills or receipts need to be submitted for per diem claims.

### 3. Key Differences
*   **Per Diem Rates:** The Onsite Handbook specifies exact dollar amounts ($250/$200) and explicitly states it includes accommodation, whereas these details are not specified for the Offshore Handbook.
*   **Booking Approvals:** For costs exceeding the lowest available fare, Onsite employees require **Department Head** approval, while Offshore employees require **VP/SVP** approval.
*   **Extension Approvals:** Stay or hotel extensions for Onsite employees require **EVP** approval, whereas Offshore employees require **Function Head** approval.
*   **Relocation/Arrival Specifics:** The Onsite Handbook contains specific details regarding travel to Singapore (2 weeks hotel, 2 days to acclimate, shipment of goods), which are not specified for Offshore travel.

### 4. Common Points
*   **Definition:** Both handbooks define business travel as a trip undertaken for work, business purposes, interviews, or knowledge transfer.
*   **Booking Responsibility:** The Affine travel team is responsible for booking the lowest available fares and lodging for both groups.
*   **Cost Justification:** Both policies require justification if an employee chooses a more expensive hotel or airfare than the one designated by the travel team.
*   **Receipt Policy:** Both policies explicitly state that no bills or receipts are required to claim per diem.
*   **Communication:** Both policies state it is the responsibility of the employee and the company to keep all parties informed in case of cancellations or changes in plans.
