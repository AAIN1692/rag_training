Based on the provided employee handbooks, the relocation benefits for employees moving from India to the US are as follows:

**Allowances and Benefits**
*   **Travel Tickets:** The Travel team books flight tickets for the employee. The process involves the Immigration Team notifying the VP/Delivery Managers of the approved visa (H-1B/L-1A/L-1B), who then notify the Travel team. Flight options must be approved by the VP before booking (Offshore Handbook, Page 10).
*   **Accommodation:** The Travel team books hotel accommodation for 7 days from the date of arrival (Offshore Handbook, Page 10).
*   **Shipment of Goods:** Relocation assistance includes the shipment of goods (Offshore Handbook, Page 10).

**Approvals**
*   **Eligibility:** Must be approved by the supervising manager, function head, and the Human Resources Department (Offshore Handbook, Page 7).
*   **Travel Approval:** The VP must approve flight booking options before the Travel team completes the booking (Offshore Handbook, Page 10).
*   **Accommodation Extensions:** Any stay extension beyond the initial 7 days must be approved by the VP (Offshore Handbook, Page 10).
*   **Exceptions:** Any exceptions to the policy regarding eligibility or extent of coverage require prior written approval from the Human Resources Department or the Head of the Department (Offshore Handbook, Page 7).
