ANSWER:
--------
According to the **Onsite Handbook** (Page 12), if an employee’s “Employment Authorization” becomes invalid or void due to any visa-related circumstances, the employee has two options:

*   **Option 1:** Resign from Affine Inc. and receive a 3-weeks’ severance.
*   **Option 2:** The employee will be terminated from Affine Inc. immediately upon notice and will be offered a full-time role at Affine Analytics Pvt. Ltd. (India Headquarters).

Additionally, the employee must leave the host country as communicated by Affine to avoid violating immigration laws (Onsite Handbook, Page 12).
