ANSWER:
--------
Based on the provided context, the salary treatment for an employee who resigns depends on their last working day and the specific handbook:

**Offshore Employees (Offshore Handbook, Page 13):**
*   **Salary Holding:** 
    *   If the last working day is between the **1st and 15th** of a month, the **previous month’s salary** will be kept on hold.
    *   If the last working day is between the **16th and the end of the month** (28th/30th/31st), the **same month’s salary** will be kept on hold.
*   **Immediate Release:** If an employee receives an immediate release, they will receive the same month’s salary along with their full and final settlement.
*   **Payment Timing:** The held salary is paid along with the "full and final settlement in [the] upcoming month."

**Onsite Employees (Onsite Handbook, Page 18):**
*   **Immediate Release Payment:** If an employee needs to be relieved immediately (rather than serving the 4-week notice period), they are required to **pay 3 weeks of salary**.
*   **Early Release Adjustment:** In the case of an early release requested by the employee, Earned Leave (EL) will be adjusted against the early days.

**General Eligibility (Onsite Handbook, Page 53):**
*   Employees serving their notice period as of March 31st are not eligible for any variable payout.
